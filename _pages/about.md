<div class="home-intro">
  <p class="home-intro__eyebrow">Ph.D. Student · University of Miami</p>
  <p class="home-intro__lead">Hello, I’m <strong>Defeng Zhou <span lang="zh">周德峰</span></strong>.</p>
  <p>I am a Ph.D. student in the <a href="https://ece.coe.miami.edu/index.html">Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering</a> at the University of Miami, advised by <a href="https://winslab.us/">Prof. Mingzhe Chen</a>. I received my B.Eng. from the College of Intelligent Systems, Shenzhen, Sun Yat-sen University in 2025, where I worked with <a href="https://scholar.google.com.sg/citations?user=AKffc_QAAAAJ&hl=en">Prof. Shimin Gong</a> in WinsLab.</p>
  <p>My research connects semantic communications with autonomous driving, machine learning, spatial reasoning, reinforcement learning, and multimodal data fusion. I am always happy to discuss new ideas and collaborations.</p>
